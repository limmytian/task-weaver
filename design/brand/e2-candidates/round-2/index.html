HUMANS + AI, SHARED WORK

Four new ways
to weave work.

Simpler initials. A little more movement. No double helix, node network or unexplained breaks.

Four continuous T/W studies. All shapes are continuous, with color and single-ink variants. These are candidates, not a shipped identity.

DIRECTION D TW study

Shared stroke

A T crossbar meets a single continuous W. One shared stroke makes the two initials act as one mark.

16 20 24 32 Actual pixel sizes

The clearest combination of both initials. Angular and energetic; the shared diagonal makes the T intentionally slanted.

DIRECTION E TW study

Easy flow

A soft W moves through two rounded turns. The short T crossbar gives the motion a starting point.

16 20 24 32 Actual pixel sizes

The softest and most fluid direction. Feels welcoming, but the T is subtler and may need stronger definition.

DIRECTION F TW study

Compact TW

A broad T and a folded W share a solid center. A compact silhouette with no cuts, crossings or loose parts.

16 20 24 32 Actual pixel sizes

The strongest single-ink silhouette and most compact construction. More structured and emblem-like than the other studies.

DIRECTION G TW study

Little tw

Lowercase initials keep a human, informal rhythm. A small upward turn gives the t a lively finish.

16 20 24 32 Actual pixel sizes

The most conversational and playful direction. Both initials remain readable; the wider shape gives up some favicon weight.
