# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect private information and avoid unwanted attention.
- Prioritize the health of the community and project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Decisions
will consider context, severity, repetition, safety, and the interests of the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[limmytian](https://github.com/limmytian) through a private contact method listed
on that profile. Do not open a public issue for sensitive conduct reports.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to
safety and legal obligations.
